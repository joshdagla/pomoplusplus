About Pomo++

A simple task management app that follows the Pomodoro Technique

Why I built this

• A lot of people might not see a point to such a simplistic project, and I would'nt blame them. There would be thousands of clones to this app available from just a single google search.

• For me however, I started learning C++ recently. As most people would know, C++ is one of the hardest languages of all time.

• So, for a starter C++ project, I wanted to touch on a domain I was already familiar with, that being Web Development. A project like Pomo++ allowed me to not worry about the domain of the project and focus on learning C++ concepts.

My Experience

• Now, would I recommend using C++ for a web dev project. Probably not. However, credit where credit is due, the folks at CrowCpp have made the entry barrier to C++ web-dev much better. Still I would not choose C++ for this.

• But it does have its own unique pros as well. It is blazingly fast, compiles down to a binary, and leaves a very small footprint. Most of all though, I had a lot of fun making this.
